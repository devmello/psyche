# S10 Channels — status (2026-10-19)

PRs: devmello/psyche#10 (`protocol:` channel additions) and the `[s10 channels]` PR from `ws/s10-channels` (stacked on #10).

## What works (M1)

- **Framework (N1).** `ChannelService` provides:
  - adapter registry, start with reconnect backoff, and health-check restarts;
  - an inbound pipeline: authorize, then pair / command / route, then `InboundSink`;
  - Markdown rendering to plain text or Telegram HTML;
  - chunking by rendered length, with code fences re-opened across chunks;
  - per-chat and per-channel token buckets, plus `rate_limited` retry;
  - typing indicators, and Library ingestion of attachments.
- **Authorization (N2).**
  - `dmPolicy`: `pairing` (default), `allowlist`, `open` (needs `"*"`), `disabled`.
  - Pairing codes: 8 characters, 1 h, at most 3 pending per channel, re-sent at most every 10 min. Approve or deny via the API; an approved sender joins `allowFrom`.
  - Owner via `ownerIds` and `commands.ownerAllowFrom`. Weak senders are never owners. Identity links, with one identity per sender.
  - Groups: group allowlist, `groupAllowFrom`, and `requireMention` from structured mentions.
  - `contextVisibility`, and command authorization.
- **Routing.**
  - `dmScope` session keys; non-owners never share `main`; identity-linked per-peer sessions; `injectMemory` only in the owner's private sessions.
  - Provenance parts: forwarded text is untrusted; quoted text keeps its author's provenance; edits are delivered as `edit`.
- **Approvals over chat (N4).**
  - Telegram buttons, or `/approve <ref> approve|deny` as a fallback.
  - Owner-only answers, always scope `once`; broker errors map to short replies.
  - Restart-invalidated and replayed answers are rejected.
  - Sanitized fixed-template cards; a card that does not fit becomes a pointer to a device.
  - **Cards per surface (design-12, closed 2026-10-19 on `ws/cards-per-surface`).** Every chat card is `renderForSurface("<channel>:<peerId>", approval)` from `@psyche/permissions/cards` plus the chat trailer (expiry, `/approve` ref), in `ChatApprovals.deliver` and `TelegramAdapter.sendApprovalButtons`. A `deviceOnly`, financial, forbidden-kind or non-opted-in `destructive` approval gets the header and "Answer this on your Mac." only (no arguments, no buttons, no ref), and is never answerable from the channel. Tests: `test/format.test.ts` (secret placeholder, untrusted snippet, device-only, credential fill) and `test/approvals.test.ts`. Question cards are not delivered to channels yet (`renderQuestionForSurface` is ready for the iMessage / M2 work).
- **WebChat (N3)** adapter. **Telegram (N4)** adapter on grammY 1.46.0, tested against hand-recorded Bot API fixtures.
- **Designated person mode (N10).** Persona overlay, tool allowlist, no `MEMORY.md`, own session, `grantSubject` for S05, approvals to the owner. Designated groups bind their member list.
- **Voice notes (N11).** Inbound voice is transcribed through `SpeechEngine`. Outbound text is synthesized once and transcoded once per container (OGG/Opus, CAF, MP3).
- **API.** `channels.*` handlers. `SqliteChannelStore` plus a migration for `channels` and `identities`.
- **Tests.** 128 vitest tests, including regressions for the 10 findings of the pre-PR security review (`pnpm --filter @psyche/channels test`), no network.

## Doc conflicts (I followed the stricter reading)

1. **Chat approval of `destructive`.** Architecture §4.6 says chat approves `write` only; security §9 says `write` and `destructive` (review design-04). Default here: `write` only. A channel can opt in with `config.allowDestructiveApprovals`. Note that `FakePermissionBroker` marks destructive as `deviceOnly` anyway.
2. **N4 "done when: the owner approves a purchase from Telegram"** contradicts security §3.7 / §9, where `financial`, purchase and credential fill are `deviceOnly`. Security wins: purchases are never delivered to or answered from chat, and `config.ownerDevice` does not change that. The feature spec row should be amended to say "approves an email send".
3. **"Ownership does not grant chat access"** (security §9) means an owner id must also pass `dmPolicy`. The owner pairs once, or is listed in `allowFrom`.
