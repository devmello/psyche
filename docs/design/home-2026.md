## 1. Critique of the old Home

Method: a design review from the screenshots and source, and a detector scan of the sources
(clean: it finds no style tells; the problems were structural). Scored 25/40.

What worked: the Ask field first; Needs you in amber holding the only blue button; honest empty
lines; the "That's it for today" end marker.

What did not:

1. **Two columns competed.** A 320 px side column (Coming up, Goals, Keep in touch, More) began
   above the fold beside the main column, so the eye had two places to start and no order.
2. **Everything was the same white card.** Seven brief posts, four suggestions, five automations,
   four goals: about 27 objects at once, all with the same weight, so no card marked an edge.
3. **Labels named things, not purposes.** "Today", "Suggestions", "More", "Goals" did not say what
   each was for or how a "Suggestion" post inside Today differs from the Suggestions section.
4. **The brief was a wall.** Suggestions started about 1,750 px down; the deciding things were
   600 px from each other.
5. **First visit was a half-empty two-column page** with three "nothing yet" placeholders.

## 2. The layout

One column, 720 px, centred. Read top to bottom; nothing sits beside it.

| # | Section | Says | Shown when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Ask {persona} anything** | the one way in; starters on a first visit | always |
| 2 | **Needs you** (amber) | approvals and questions, "All notifications" | something waits |
| 3 | **Pinned** | live widgets you pinned | any pinned |
| 4 | **Today**: "Your brief, written each morning" | Catching up, then the posts (three, then "Show N more") | a brief exists |
| 5 | **Suggestions**: "Ideas and goals for you to say yes or no to" | two, then "Show N more" | any |
| 6 | **Coming up**: "The next runs of your automations and routines" | three | any scheduled |
| 7 | **Goals**: "What you are working toward" | tracked goals | any tracked |
| 8 | **Keep in touch**: "People who may be waiting to hear from you" | two nudges | any |
| 9 | footer links | Wren & you, Scheduled, Inbox, Brief settings, Soul & Memory, Memory review | always |

The order is urgency, then the day, then what is next: what must be decided now (2), what you
chose to watch (3), what happened (4), what could be done (5), what is scheduled and tracked
(6 to 8).

Rules:

- **Edges.** A hairline and 40 px above and below each section; a section that is not drawn leaves
  no rule, because the rule belongs to the section that follows it (a CSS sibling rule, so no
  section needs to know about its neighbours). Section titles are 17 px (they were 15 px, the same
  as the card titles under them). Cards stay only around items you can act on and around lists
  of rows; a section is never itself a card.
- **Purpose line.** Every section but Needs you and Pinned has one grey line under its title.
- **Empty sections are not drawn**: Today (until a brief exists or is being written), Coming up,
  Goals, Keep in touch, Suggestions, Pinned, Needs you. Load errors still show, as one line with
  "Try again". The first visit is the Ask field, the starters and the footer.
- **Short lists say so.** "Show 4 more" / "Show less" (`aria-expanded`) under the brief and the
  suggestions. The end marker shows once the whole brief is open.
- **One accent.** Unchanged: the first Approve when something waits, otherwise the first idea's
  Do it. Amber is only the Needs you dot. The footer and "Show more" are quiet buttons.
- **Footer, not a list.** "More" was a card of five rows with hints; it is now one wrapped line
  of plain links (the hints are tooltips). It gained "Brief settings" (`/home/instructions`),
  because an empty brief is no longer drawn and its Instructions, Schedule and "Generate the
  first brief" live in that sheet. Reaching Add a goal from an empty Goals section is by ⌘K, and
  Goals draws its "+" once there is one.

Narrow (under about 720 px of content) is the same column with less padding: there is no second
layout.

Not changed: what each section is made of (`NeedsYouList`, `FeedPostCard`, `IdeaCard`,
`GoalRow`, nudges, pinned widgets), the Overview, People (it shares `KeepInTouchSection` and
gains its purpose line), the sidebar, the gateway and the protocol.

## 3. Today and "Catching up"

The memory-for-everything work will add a catch-up part to the daily brief: projects,
assignments, due work and mail ("your essay is due Thursday, you finished the outline
yesterday"). It is the first thing in Today, above the posts, because it is the part of the
brief that is about the owner's own commitments; the posts are what the world did.

```
Today   7 posts                      [What should I write about] [Schedule]
Your brief, written each morning
  Catching up   Projects, assignments and mail since you last looked
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Essay on the printing press                          Due Thu > │
  │ You finished the outline yesterday. Drafting is next. · Canvas │
  │ ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │
  │ Lab 4 write-up                                  Due Mon, Oct 26│
  │ Your TA posted feedback on lab 3 an hour ago. · Canvas         │
  │ …                                                              │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  [Briefing post] [post] [post]   Show 4 more
```

- `CatchUp` (`packages/ui/src/home/CatchUp.tsx`) takes `CatchUpItem[]`: `id`, `kind`
  (project, assignment, mail, task), `title`, `line` (what changed or what is next, one sentence),
  optional `dueAt`, `source` and `route`. Dated rows come first, soonest first; at most three.
- It is drawn through `HomeScreen`'s `catchUp` prop, which `FeedScreen` hands to the brief as its
  `lead`. Nothing supplies the prop yet: there is **no data plumbing**, only the slot, the
  component and the stories `Screens/Home / Catching Up` and `Catching Up Alone` (fixture data).
- A due date is ink text at the row's end, weightier within two days, **never amber**: amber
  means "needs you", and a due essay is not an approval.
- With catch-up rows and no posts, Today is drawn for the catch-up alone.
- The planning session decides the real source and may rename `kind`; the layout only needs
  rows, a title and a sentence.

## 4. What changed in the code

- `HomeScreen`: one column; sections in the order above; `catchUp` prop.
- `sections.tsx`: Suggestions, Coming up, Goals and Keep in touch take the purpose line and a
  larger title; Coming up and Goals draw nothing when empty; `MoreSection` is the footer.
- `FeedView` / `FeedScreen`: `lead`, `collapseAfter` and `hideWhenEmpty`.
- `ScreenSection`: `description` and `large`. `components/show-more.tsx`: the Show more button.
- Copy: new `home.*` keys (purpose lines, Show more, Catching up, Brief settings, Memory review);
  `home.side` and `home.goals.empty` are gone.
- Baselines refreshed: Home (all stories), Shell layout Home stories, Feed screen stories,
  Cards / Workflows "In the feed" and Specialists / Results "Feed" (the brief's title is larger
  and it has a purpose line).

## 5. Tests

`packages/ui/test/home.test.tsx` covers the order of the sections and that no complementary
landmark remains, the three-post and two-suggestion limits with Show more, an empty brief left
out with Brief settings reachable, Catching up inside Today sorted by due date, and the first
visit being the Ask field and the footer. Every Home story runs through axe in
`test/stories.test.tsx`.

## 6. Open

- "Coming up" still lists machine routines such as "Heartbeat every 45 min" next to things in
  the owner's life; filtering them to user-meaningful runs is a data decision, left alone.
- Brief posts of kind "suggestion" still sit in Today as well as in Suggestions. Moving them is
  a gateway decision.
- Keep in touch cards carry four actions each; collapsing Snooze, Done and Not this person into
  an overflow is worth doing on People too, so it was left for a pass on both.
