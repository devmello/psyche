features:
  - title: Runs where your data is
    details: One Gateway on your Mac or a Linux server holds your threads, memory, files and credentials in a single directory you control. No telemetry.
  - title: Built to do real work
    details: A browser of its own, documents and files, tasks and automations, chat channels, connectors, the coding helper, and computer use on your Mac. Each page says what works today.
  - title: Asks before it acts
    details: Every action that changes something goes through the Permission Broker. Approval cards show exactly what will happen, and untrusted content can never approve anything.
  - title: Secrets stay out of the model
    details: Passwords and keys live in an encrypted vault and are filled at the last step. The model only sees placeholders.
