# Psyche points

Psyche points helps you fill in a form or page in another app, such as a visa form in Safari or a PDF in Preview. A small ring appears around the next field, with a short note beside it. You type; Psyche only points.

It is off until you turn it on in Settings → Computer use → Psyche points. It works on your Mac, from the desktop app.

## Use it

1. Press ⌘K and choose **Point me through a form**, or ask in Chat ("help me fill this in") and press **Point me through it**.
2. Pick the window and, if you like, say what you are trying to do.
3. Psyche points at the first field. Fill it in and the ring moves to the next one. **Skip**, **Back** and **Next** are on the note.

If macOS asks, allow Accessibility for Psyche Helper. Screen Recording is asked only if you press **Let Psyche look** on a page where Psyche cannot read the fields.

## What it reads

- The window you picked: the app name, the title, the page address (without anything after the `?`), headings, and each field's label and options.
- What you type into ordinary fields, so it can tell what is left to do.
- It sends this to your chosen model provider to plan the steps. With a model that runs on your Mac, it stays on your Mac.

## What it never reads

- Passwords, one-time codes, card, bank and ID numbers. For those fields Psyche never reads what is in them, never suggests a value and never sends them anywhere. The note just says "You fill this yourself".
- Other windows and other apps.
- A picture of the window, unless you press **Let Psyche look**. Even then, sensitive fields are painted over first, and the picture is not saved.
- Banking apps, password managers, Terminal, System Settings and Psyche itself are never offered.

## What it cannot do

It never clicks, types, scrolls or presses a key for you. On a payment page, the Pay button note tells you to check the amount and decide yourself.

Nothing about the form is kept after you stop: no Memory, no saved screenshots. The thread you started from gets one line saying how many pages and fields it pointed you through.

## Stop

Press **Esc** at any time, or press **Stop** on the note, or choose Stop pointing in the menu bar. Psyche also stops by itself if the window closes, the helper quits or you leave it idle.
