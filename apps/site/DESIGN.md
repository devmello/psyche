# Design System: Psyche Docs

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Notebook of Files"**

The site is the app's ink world set down on a desk. Every surface is a sheet of paper: the landing page is an open notebook with a two-page spread, then four sheets dealt onto the desk for what only Psyche does, one index sheet for the rest, then the status leaf and the close; the doc pages are the same paper, read flat. Paper is a warm-neutral gray (hue 80, almost no chroma), text is near-black ink, and one ink blue carries every link, button, source mark and the step being shown. Amber appears only when something needs you.

The world is literal about what Psyche is: memory and receipts are plain files, so pages are headed by the file they are about, set in mono, and every use case closes by citing the doc page it was drawn from with a numbered source chip, the way Psyche cites its sources. Proof is the real app: each stage holds a recording of Psyche's own UI on fictional fixture data, framed like a print pinned to the page, and the scroll is its playhead. An iPhone recording is shown as its own screen capture, with the screen's corners and no drawn device. Nothing is illustrated or invented.

Night is a material change, not an inversion. Doc pages go to true dark grays. The landing page keeps every sheet as lamplit paper on a dark desk, and the Face ID stage is one lamplit sheet on a dark night band under a single warm pool of light, in either theme.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm-neutral paper grays, one ink accent per view, amber reserved for "needs you".
- Display serif (New York first) for titles at weight 500 with tight negative tracking; system sans for reading; mono only for real paths, ids and commands.
- Sheets with soft, layered offset shadows and a 1px margin rule; no borders on sheets.
- Running heads are real file paths only; demo stages carry none.
- Numbered source chips close each use case and head each index entry, linking its doc page.
- Demos are real recordings scrubbed by the scroll: on a wide, tall screen each stage pins and the clip moves only as you scroll, backwards too, with its steps lighting as it reaches them. Index plates play on hover (in view on touch). Nothing idles.

## Colors

Near-neutral paper with one saturated blue and a reserved amber; the palette is intentionally almost monochrome so the ink reads as meaning.

### Primary
- **Notebook Ink** (`ink`, the brand #2c5aa0): primary buttons, focus rings, the active sidebar tick, the outline marker, the active nav underline, the current step's tick on a demo stage, `accent-color` and caret. Hover deepens to **Pressed Ink** (`ink-hover`).
- **Ink Text** (`ink-text`): links, source chips, doc-page citations. In dark doc pages it lifts to **Moonlit Ink** (`night-ink-text`) for contrast; on lamplit sheets it darkens slightly (`lit-ink-text`).
- **Ink Wash** (`ink-soft`, `ink-soft-2`): source-chip fill, selected search result, text selection, highlighted code lines.
- **Ink Rule** (`ink-line`): resting underline of links, hover border of the pager.

### Secondary
- **Needs-You Amber** (`amber`, `amber-line`, `amber-text`, `amber-soft`): warning callouts (1px inset amber ring on amber-soft), and the amber ring and amber running head of the approval sheet in the hero and closing stacks. Nothing else.

### Neutral
- **Paper ramp** (`paper-1` to `paper-12`, the app's gray-1..12 at hue 80): `paper-1` page background, `paper-2` sidebar and code blocks, `paper-3` inline code and search field, `paper-5` hairlines, dividers, step rules, index entry rules and the timeline track, `paper-6`/`paper-7` borders, button outlines and the clip and plate ring, `paper-9` faint marks and steps not yet reached, `paper-11` secondary text, reached steps, stage captions and index lines, `paper-12` primary text, the current step and the timeline fill.
- **Sheet** (`sheet`): the brightest paper, for leaves, demo stages, the index sheet, the notebook, pager cards and the terminal.
- **Desk** (`desk`): the surface the landing sheets lie on; the landing nav sits on it too.
- **Desk at Night** (`night-paper-1`, `night-sheet`, `night-desk`, `night-paper-12`): the doc theme's true dark grays under `.dark`.
- **Night Band** (`night-band`, deepening to `night-band-dark` in dark mode): the full-bleed band behind the Face ID stage, and the nav's color, full width, while the band is under it.
- **Lamplit Paper** (`lit-sheet` and the `lit-*` overrides): the paper a sheet becomes under the lamp, a step dimmer than daylight sheet with darker secondary text so small type stays legible.

### Named Rules
**The One Ink Rule.** One accent per view, and it is ink. Ink marks what you can act on or follow (links, buttons, sources, focus) and the one step the recording is showing; it never decorates.

**The Amber Means You Rule.** Amber appears only where the owner's decision is required. It is never a highlight, a badge color or a second accent.

**The Lamplit Paper Rule.** On the landing page, paper stays paper at night: in dark mode, and on the night band in either mode, sheets take the `lit-*` overrides instead of going dark. The overrides live in the landing's unscoped style block so they reach every `.lit` sheet, the stages included. Doc pages are the exception; they use the true dark ramp.

## Typography

**Display Font:** ui-serif, resolving to New York on Apple systems, then Iowan Old Style, then self-hosted Source Serif 4 (variable, optical size), then Charter and Georgia.
**Body Font:** system-ui (SF Pro Text on Apple systems) with Segoe UI, Roboto and Helvetica Neue fallbacks.
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace (SF Mono), JetBrains Mono, Menlo, Consolas.

**Character:** A bookish serif at a modest weight over a plain system sans: the notebook's titles are handwritten-careful, the text is the operating system's own voice. Mono is evidence, not decoration.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, clamp(44px, 6.3vw, 96px), 0.97, -0.028em, max 11ch): the hero headline on the notebook's left page only.
- **Headline** (500, clamp(32px, 3.7vw, 54px), 1.04, -0.022em, max 17ch): landing leaf titles (status); the closing title steps up to clamp(36px, 4.6vw, 66px).
- **Stage Headline** (500, clamp(30px, 3.2vw, 48px), 1.05, -0.022em, max 14ch): the h2 of each demo stage. The centred layout (currently unused) steps down to clamp(28px, 2.5vw, 38px); pinned at 900px tall or less it sets at clamp(28px, 4.6svh, 40px).
- **Doc Headline** (500, clamp(34px, 4vw, 44px), 1.1, -0.02em): doc page h1.
- **Title** (500, 27px, 1.2, -0.012em): doc h2, with a 1px paper-5 rule above. Doc h3 is 20px; landing column heads 22px; the nav wordmark 21px; sidebar groups 15.5px; the close's docs index 18 to 19px. All in the display serif.
- **Index Title** (500, 21px, 1.2, -0.01em): an index-sheet entry's h3, paper-12, led by its source chip. Its line under it is 15px, 1.5, paper-11, 34em max, with real paths in mono.
- **Deck** (400, clamp(17px, 1.45vw, 21px), 1.5, max 34em): the hero's two-sentence deck, in paper-11. A stage's one-line deck is body size (16.5px, 1.55, paper-11), 15.5px when pinned on a short screen and 15px in the centred stage.
- **Body** (400, 16.5px, 1.7, 72ch on doc pages; 17.5px, 1.6, 36em on landing leaves): reading text, secondary copy in paper-11.
- **Step** (400, 15px, 1.45): a demo stage's steps; 14.5px with 7px row padding when pinned at 900px tall or less.
- **Label** (400 to 550, 12.5 to 15.5px): status lines, the stage caption (12.5px, paper-11, for contrast), margin notes, button text (15.5px, 550).
- **Mono Head** (400, 12.5px): running heads (the index sheet's is `docs/03-feature-spec.md`), citation paths. Real paths inside steps and index lines (`MEMORY.md`, `~/.psyche`) are mono at 0.88em on a paper-3 chip with a paper-5 hairline.

### Named Rules
**The Serif Is For Titles Rule.** The display serif sets headings, the wordmark, sidebar groups and a few row labels, always at 500. Running text, steps, buttons and captions stay in the sans.

**The Mono Is Evidence Rule.** Mono only for a real file path, id, time or command. A running head names the file the page is about (`README.md`, `docs/release-notes/0.3.md`); a section with no real file has no running head, which is why demo stages have none.

## Layout

The landing page is a desk with a 1480px max reading width and a fluid side gutter (`gutter-x`, 12px on phones). The first viewport is an open book: two equal pages split by a 1px gutter line with a soft 80px fold gradient, pinned while the hero stack fans (desktop only, 961px+ wide and 640px+ tall; static under reduced motion). Below it are four lead demo stages, one original capability each, separated by `leaf-gap`: the Live Activity in the Dynamic Island (iPhone), cited answers (Search), the Face ID payment (iPhone, on the night band under the lamp) and teach by showing. Inside a stage the copy (an h2, a one-line deck, four steps, a source link) sits beside the recording in a 4:8 split with `stage-gap` between. Stages alternate their `from` side; on desktop the recording sits on that side, so the clip changes sides stage by stage. Every stage uses the side layout; the centred layout still exists in the component but no stage uses it. Then comes the index sheet: a leaf headed `docs/03-feature-spec.md` holding the other six capabilities as a contents page (see Components). After it come the status leaf (two columns, "Also works today" and "Not yet") and the close, a centered 980px column ending in a five-column docs index.

A stage whose recording is an iPhone screen is a phone stage. Pinned, its copy and phone sit together in the middle of the sheet: a centred two-column body (copy `minmax(260px, 500px)`, then the phone at its own width) with a column gap of clamp(56px, 9vw, 160px), mirrored when dealt from the left. The phone is `min(360px, (100svh - nav - 190px) × ratio)` wide. Unpinned it is `min(320px, 100%)`, centred, and its caption wraps at 24em.

**The Contents Page Rule.** The lead stages are for what only Psyche does. Everything else it does goes on the one index sheet, as numbered entries with a plate each; never as a grid of feature cards and never as another stage.

Pinned (961px+ wide and 760px+ tall), a stage's sheet fills the screen under the nav (`100svh - nav - 28px`, 14px below the nav) and its track is `100svh + duration × 6svh + 20svh` tall. The recording is as large as the sheet allows: the minimum of 62% of the page, the room the copy column leaves (`min(100vw, 1480px) - 19vw - 310px`) and the sheet's height times the clip's ratio; the copy takes the rest (at least 260px). At 900px tall or less the copy is set tighter.

At 960px and below every split collapses to one column, the book becomes a single page and the hero stack gets a fixed height. A stage then reads in order: heading, deck, the recording, the steps, the link. At 560px and below the gutter drops to 12px, sheet radii tighten to 4px, stage padding drops to 16px 14px 20px and its margin rule goes, the clip switches to its phone take, the docs index stacks and buttons fill the line. The index sheet's contents run three columns, two at 960px and below, one at 560px and below. Nothing scrolls horizontally.

Doc pages keep VitePress's frame at a 1400px layout max, with 72ch measure for paragraphs and lists.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is paper on a desk: soft, layered, offset-down shadows with three stops (contact, near, far), warm-tinted in light mode and pure black at night. Sheets are borderless; the shadow is their edge. Lift is a state: the notebook spread and the search modal float on the lift shadow; laid sheets rest on the sheet shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sheet** (`--shadow-sheet`): resting leaves, demo stages, the terminal, hero sheets.
- **Lift** (`--shadow-lift`): the notebook spread, the search modal.
- **Clip** (a 1px paper-6 ring, then two warm stops): the recording's frame, printed onto its sheet. An index plate takes the same ring with a slightly shorter far stop (14px 30px -20px).
- **Screen** (a 1.5px oklch(0.25 0.004 80) hairline, then two warm stops to 0.55): an iPhone recording's edge, the screen's own dark rim and nothing more.
- **Lamplit** (three black stops to 0.8 alpha): the Face ID stage on the night band, easing toward a daylight shadow (to 22% strength) as the band leaves.
- **Primary button** (two small ink-tinted stops): the only shadow on a control.

### Named Rules
**The Laid Down Rule.** Stages are dealt onto the desk by the scroll: as a stage's top edge rises through the viewport (`--e`) its sheet slides in 6% from its `from` side, rises 14svh and straightens from a 2.2deg turn on a cubic ease, and its copy settles a beat later (opacity 0.15 to 1, 18px rise). Leaving (`--x`), it is set aside, shrinking up to 5%. Reduced motion and no-script show every sheet flat.

**The Lamp Rule.** Night is one band and one lamp. The band darkens from the desk as it arrives and the desk fades back in at its lower edge as it leaves; a single warm radial pool hangs inside the pinned stage over its upper left, at the band's darkness (`--dark`).

## Shapes

Paper has barely rounded corners: 6px for leaves, stages and the notebook (4px on phones), 3px for the hero's small sheets. Controls and framed objects are rounder: 10px buttons, recordings, code blocks, callouts and pager cards; 8px index plates; 12px for the terminal; 14px for the search modal; 5px chips and inline code; 4px for path chips in steps; a 2px-radius 7px square for a step's tick. An iPhone recording keeps the screen's own elliptical corners (13% / 6%). The only pill is the clip's play control. Lines are 1px hairlines throughout: margin rules, running-head rules, step rules, dividers. The timeline is a 2px hairline with 2×8px ticks. The single 2px stroke is the ink focus ring.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet and solid, never loud.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), 46px tall, 20px side padding, 15.5px at weight 550, 9px icon gap.
- **Primary:** ink fill, white text, a small ink-tinted shadow. Hover deepens to pressed ink and lifts 1px; active returns flat.
- **Quiet:** sheet fill with a paper-7 hairline and paper-12 text; hover darkens the border to paper-9 and fills paper-2.
- **Small control:** the terminal's Copy button, 7px radius, paper-2 fill, paper-6 border, 13px.
- **Focus:** a 2px ink outline at 2px offset, everywhere.

### Source Chip
The site's signature citation. Each demo stage ends with a link: a numbered chip (20px square minimum, 5px radius, ink-soft fill, 12px mono numeral in ink-text), the doc page's title in ink-text at 550, and the doc path in 12.5px mono paper-9. Numbers run in page order: 1 to 4 on the stages, continuing 5 to 10 on the index sheet's entries. Hover underlines the title only.

### Demo Stage
The landing page's unit: one use case, shown by the real app doing it.
- **Sheet:** a lamplit-capable sheet (6px, sheet shadow, the margin rule) padded `stage-pad-y` / `stage-pad-x`, with no running head and no eyebrow.
- **Copy:** the stage headline, a one-line deck in paper-11, three to five steps, and the source link.
- **Steps:** an ordered list ruled with paper-5 hairlines; each step is a button with a 7px square tick. Steps not yet reached are paper-9 with an outlined tick; reached steps are paper-11 with a filled paper-11 tick; the current step is paper-12 and its tick fills ink and grows 25%, the view's one accent. Hover takes a step to paper-12. Each carries hidden "Show in the recording:" text and `aria-current="step"` when current. Choosing a step scrolls to its moment when pinned and seeks the clip otherwise. With reduced motion no step leads, and all read at paper-12.
- **Recording:** a clip frame (below), then a 2px paper-5 timeline 14px under it: a paper-12 fill scaled to the playhead, and a 2×8px tick per step in paper-7 that turns paper-12 once reached. The timeline is hidden with reduced motion.
- **Caption:** 12.5px paper-11 (for contrast), "<place in the app>, recorded from Psyche's own UI on fictional data."
- **Variants:** `from="left"` or `"right"` sets the side it is dealt from and the side its recording sits on; `phone` marks an iPhone recording: the phone-stage layout (see Layout) and the screen frame on its clip; `lamp` hangs the night band's lamp inside its sticky stage. `layout="center"` (the recording centred, the copy in a 4fr/7fr row beneath) is still in the component but unused.

### Clip Frame and Scrubbing
A real recording of the app framed as a print: 10px radius, a paper-6 ring and the clip shadow over the app's own window background, so the frame never flashes desk color while loading.
- **Scrubbed** (pinned only): the scroll maps to the playhead. The first 4% of the track holds the first frame, the next 88% scrubs, the last 8% holds the last frame. Seeks are throttled: while one is in flight only the newest target is kept, applied on `seeked`. The poster is `<name>-start.webp`, the first frame. There is no play control.
- **Played** (unpinned): muted, looping, once 45% visible, one clip at a time across the page through a shared registry; it holds still while the page scrolls and resumes 180ms after it rests. The poster is `<name>.webp`, the key moment. A pill Play/Pause control (32px, 13px text) shows on hover, on focus, when paused and always on touch.
- **Phone take:** `<name>-phone.mp4` (and its poster) at 560px and below, framed to its own ratio so app text stays legible. The Mac-app clips have one (brief, answer, memory, files, devices, teach, skill); the iPhone takes (540×1174 portrait) need none, being phone-shaped already.
- **iPhone screen:** an iPhone recording is framed as the screen it is: the screen's corner radius (13% / 6%), the 1.5px dark hairline and the screen shadow, over #f9f8f6 so it never flashes while loading. No device is drawn around it.
- **Manual** (index plates): with a fine pointer, hovering the entry starts the clip and leaving stops it; the Play/Pause control stays in the page, hidden until the plate is hovered or the control is focused, so keyboard and click work too. On touch it plays in view, one at a time. With reduced motion the control shows and the clip plays only on Play.
- **Reduced motion:** nothing pinned, the key-moment poster, and Play only on request.
- **Loading:** nothing loads until the clip is within 120% of a viewport; scrubbed clips preload fully, played clips metadata only.
- **Encoding:** H.264 MP4 only. Scrub takes (desktop) have a 1s GOP at 24fps, CRF 31 to 33, so any frame is a short decode away; play takes (phone) have a 2s GOP. Each poster has a `.webp.json` sidecar naming its origin: a Storybook story in `packages/ui` captured with Playwright (the shared-skill take is `site-demos--shared-skill`); the iOS app's Debug build on `-psycheScenario live` fixtures, recorded with `simctl io recordVideo` on an iPhone 18 Pro simulator with the status bar set to 9:41 (the iPhone takes, and half of the devices composite); or, for teach, the real `apps/web` TeachReview component in a temporary, uncommitted harness on a fictional capture.

**The Own Screen Rule.** A phone recording is shown as its own capture, edged by the screen's corners and rim. Never draw a device frame, bezel or notch around it.

## Motion

One engine, `scroll-scenes.ts`, writes `--p` (0 → 1) on each `[data-scene]` element per animation frame (rects read first, then written). CSS derives every transform, clip-path and opacity from `--p` with `clamp()` windows (`--k: clamp(0, (p - start) / length, 1)`), so motion is scrubbed by the scroll and reversible.

- **Flow scenes** (`data-scene="flow"`) run while the element rises from the bottom of the viewport to `data-end` (default 0.4 of the viewport).
- **Enter** (`data-enter`) adds `--e`, 0 → 1 as the element's top edge rises from the bottom of the viewport to the top, so a stage is dealt before it pins.
- **Exit** (`data-exit`) adds `--x`, 0 → 1 as the element's bottom edge leaves the viewport; stages are set aside with it, and the night band uses it to let daylight rise back from its lower edge and to fade the lamp and its shadow.
- **Emit** (`data-emit`) also dispatches a `scene` event with `{ p }` whenever `--p` changes, for a scene whose script needs the number: a demo stage feeds it to its clip's playhead.
- **Pin scenes** (`data-scene="pin"`) are tall tracks with a sticky stage, pinned only at 961px+ wide and 760px+ tall; a demo stage's track is sized by its clip's duration (see Layout). Elsewhere they run over their own height.
- **Default `--p: 1`** is the finished state, so no script and `prefers-reduced-motion` show every scene complete, unpinned.
- **Sequences:** each stage is dealt from its side and its clip scrubs with the scroll; the index sheet deals its entries in turn (`--q` with a per-entry `--i` offset); the night band darkens from the desk and the lamp comes up with it; "Also works today" rows ink from pencil to ink and check off in order while "Not yet" stays in pencil; the hero's four sheets fan with the spread and return fanned at the close to settle into a pile; install lines type by whole characters (`round()` on `ch`).
- **Budget:** only transform, opacity and clip-path change per frame, plus video seeks throttled to one in flight. Played clips run one at a time and hold still while the page scrolls, which keeps scrolling at 60fps.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use ink for every actionable or cited thing and the one current step, and nothing else; one accent per view.
- **Do** keep landing sheets as lamplit paper in dark mode, and put doc pages on the true dark ramp.
- **Do** head a sheet with the real file it is about, in mono, or leave it without a head.
- **Do** close each demo stage with a numbered source chip linking its doc page.
- **Do** show the product with real recordings of Psyche's UI on fictional fixture data, each with a first-frame poster (`<name>-start.webp`), a key-moment poster (`<name>.webp`), a phone take unless the recording is already phone-shaped, and a `.webp.json` provenance sidecar.
- **Do** encode scrubbed takes as H.264 with a 1s GOP so the scroll can seek anywhere.
- **Do** set titles in the display serif at 500 with negative tracking; body in the system sans.
- **Do** give small metadata text at least 4.5:1 against its sheet; the lamplit overrides already darken paper-9 to `lit-paper-9` for this.
- **Do** put what only Psyche does on the lead stages, and everything else on the index sheet.
- **Do** give every motion a static fallback under `prefers-reduced-motion` and a no-WebGL HTML stack for the hero.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use amber for anything but "needs you".
- **Don't** put topic labels or eyebrows above titles; running heads are file paths only, and stages carry none.
- **Don't** turn code blocks into dark slabs or give sheets borders; paper is edged by its shadow.
- **Don't** use mono for prose, labels or decoration.
- **Don't** invert the landing sheets to dark paper at night.
- **Don't** use sparkles or other AI glyphs, testimonials, counts or illustrated proof.
- **Don't** let anything idle or loop for attention; a clip plays only while it is the one in view, and a scrubbed clip moves only with the scroll.
- **Don't** draw device frames around phone recordings; the screen's own corners and rim are the edge.
- **Don't** lay out secondary features as a grid of feature cards; they go on the contents page.
- **Don't** give a stage to a capability every agent has.
- **Don't** claim more for the iPhone app than main does: Live Activities update while the app is open (closed-app updates need the owner's own Apple developer key), and Face ID on the phone runs on the app's demo data until the phone presence key ships.
