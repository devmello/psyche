Search: Contacts is a source (name, nickname, company, job title, emails, numbers, city and country, birthday; never street addresses, notes or groups; off by default and off for the agent until allowed; read through the Host's own Mac helper, collapses onto the person's Memory entry), and "Index on battery" (`search.runOnBattery`, off by default) lets backfills, first reads, Rebuild and the Library's OCR backfill run on battery at half the pace. Low Power Mode still pauses them, and a refused Rebuild now names the real reason (Low Power Mode, or battery with the setting off).
