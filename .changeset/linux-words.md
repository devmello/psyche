Linux has its own wording. The app no longer says "this Mac", "Touch ID", "Finder" or "System Settings" on Linux: it says "this computer", "your password" (the account password, asked through the system dialog), "Files" and "Settings", with Ctrl shortcuts and the system tray. The UI platform now has a `linux` value (the desktop's `data-platform="linux"` and Linux browsers), with Linux variants of every message that names a Mac in English and all ten languages. The Gateway's host words (`hostWords`) give the same Linux words in errors, approval cards and notifications on a Linux host.
