Search and ⌘K show an answer card when the query reads like a question: a short answer from reviewed facts, with a citation chip per claim that opens the fact and shows when Psyche learned it, and a plain reason when there is no answer. `memory.life.answer` numbers its citations by the facts it returns, and facts carry `learnedAt`.
