Relationship nudges can suggest up to three things to bring up with the person (for example "Ask how the new job at Initech is going"), each with the fact or promise it comes from. Only accepted, non-sensitive facts and your own promises are used; with too little known there are none. Draft a reply passes them to the chat as context. An optional, default-off setting rewords them with the model chosen for everything memory.
