5 heat pump myths, busted

Climate Tech Digest, 22 July 2026

  1. "They stop working when it freezes." Modern cold-climate models keep heating well below -20 °C, with lower efficiency.
  2. "You can rip out your furnace on day one." Most cold-climate installs keep some backup heat for the coldest nights.
  3. "They are only for new houses." Retrofits are common, but air sealing first pays off.
  4. "Defrost means it's broken." Periodic defrost is normal in humid cold weather.
  5. "Bigger is better." Oversized units short-cycle; size to a heat-loss calculation.
