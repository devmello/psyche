Cold-climate heat pumps: buyer's guide

Updated 1 August 2026 by the Northern Energy Agency.

What counts as "cold-climate"

To qualify for agency programs, a heat pump must deliver a COP of at least 1.75 at -15 °C and keep at least 70% of rated capacity at that temperature.

Sizing and installation

  • Size to your local design temperature and keep a backup heat source for the coldest nights.
  • Mount the outdoor unit at least 30 cm above the typical snow line, with a path for defrost water.
  • Ask the installer for a room-by-room heat-loss calculation; rules of thumb oversize systems.

Incentives

The Home Electrification Credit covers 30% of installed cost, up to $2,000, for qualifying equipment installed in 2026. Utility rebates can be combined with it.
