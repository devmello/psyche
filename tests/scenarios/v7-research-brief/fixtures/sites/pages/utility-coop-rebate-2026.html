Heat pump rebate (2026 program)

Members who install a qualifying cold-climate heat pump receive $1,000 per ton of heating capacity, up to $5,000 per home.

  • Equipment must appear on the agency's cold-climate qualified list.
  • Apply within 90 days of installation with the installer's invoice.
  • Heat-pump homes can choose the winter heat rate of 9.8¢/kWh from November through March.

The 2025 program has closed; its page has been retired.
