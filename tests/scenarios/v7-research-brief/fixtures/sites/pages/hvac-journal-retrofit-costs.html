What cold-climate heat pump retrofits cost in 2026

HVAC Journal, 14 September 2026

We surveyed 310 completed residential installs across northern states and provinces between January and August 2026.

  • Whole-home ducted systems: median $18,500 installed (middle 80%: $14,000 to $24,000).
  • Ductless mini-splits: about $4,800 per indoor head, including line sets and electrical work.
  • Electrical panel upgrades were needed in 22% of homes and added $2,500 on average.
  • Simple payback before incentives: 7 to 12 years when replacing oil or propane, 15 years or more when replacing natural gas.

Installers said the most common callback was undersized condensate drainage that froze during defrost.
