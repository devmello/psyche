Two winters, 42 homes: how cold-climate heat pumps actually performed

Cold Climate Housing Lab, research report CCHL-2026-03, published 10 March 2026

Method

We metered 42 occupied single-family homes with ducted and ductless cold-climate air-source heat pumps through the 2024-25 and 2025-26 heating seasons. Design temperatures ranged from -25 °C to -32 °C. Every home kept an electric-resistance or fossil backup.

Findings

Outdoor temperatureMeasured COP (median)
+8 °C3.1
-8 °C2.4
-15 °C1.9
-25 °C1.5
  • Seasonal COP across all homes: 2.6.
  • Backup heat supplied 4% of annual heating energy; almost all of it on nights below -25 °C.
  • Heating energy cost fell 48% versus propane and 41% versus heating oil at 2025-26 regional prices.
  • Defrost cycles cost about 8% of output in humid weather between -5 and +2 °C.

Caveats

Homes were weatherized before install. Results for leaky houses are likely worse; size equipment to the design temperature, not the average winter day.
